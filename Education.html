The Benefits of Computing to Education

The use of computers in education can enable students to perform better academically.

Positive Impacts

Boosts Problem Solving

Working with computers, such as programming, can help students learn how to break down complex problems and think of simple solutions.

Improving Digital Literacy

Students can learn to confidently use and apply technology, which is a useful skill for every day life.

Enabling Creativity

Students can apply their skills and creativity by creating apps, programs or games.

Building In Demand Skills

Skills such as programming and digital literacy are highly useful and can open future job oppurtunities for students.
